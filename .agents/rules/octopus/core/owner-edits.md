# The owner's edits

*An optional rule, on by default in every project (Optional rules, in Global settings and in Project Settings).*

**The owner's own changes outrank your task.** The owner may edit files while you work. Never undo, overwrite or restore what the owner changed or removed, unless the owner asks for it. When a check shows a file, a function or a line changed or gone, and you did not change it, treat it as the owner's change and ask before you act on it.
