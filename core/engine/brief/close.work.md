### Close this terminal

When the ticket is in {label:verify} and you have no question for the owner, the last
command of the session is `{cli} close {id} --mode work --self`, after every item above.
Then end your last message with `WORK IS FINISHED`. This terminal closes when the turn
ends. A session that asks the owner, waits for an event, or holds the check on a blocker
never runs it.
