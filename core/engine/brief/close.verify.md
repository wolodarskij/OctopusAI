### Close this terminal

When the ticket is in {label:closed} or back in {label:start} and you have no question for the
owner, the last command of the session is `{cli} close {id} --mode verify --self`, after every
item above. Then end your last message with `WORK IS FINISHED`. This terminal closes when the
turn ends. A session that asks the owner or waits for an event never runs it.
